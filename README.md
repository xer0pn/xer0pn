## Projects

### DTAI — My Personal Trading System

> I'm building DTAI for one reason: I want a system that makes money
> while I sleep. Not a side project — this is something I'm building
> for myself, because the alternative is leaving money on the table.
> It watches crypto and stock markets, figures out what the market is
> doing right now, then decides how much to bet and which strategy to
> run. If it starts losing, it stops itself. Everything is configurable,
> everything is measured.
>
> The end goal is to make it fully agentic — a system that doesn't just
> follow rules but reasons about the market and makes decisions on its own.

`Python` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `Binance` `Alpaca` `Multi-strategy` `Risk Management`
